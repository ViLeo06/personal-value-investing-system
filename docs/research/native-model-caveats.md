# Native model caveats

These Gate 0 outputs are valuation-system validation artifacts. They are not personalized investment advice and are not production allocations.

The calculations are highly sensitive to growth, margin, RONIC, capital requirements, discount rates, stable-state assumptions, the equity bridge and historical market data. Provisional evidence automatically blocks capital allocation in the decision layer.

The purpose of freezing uncomfortable outputs is to test whether the architecture preserves assumptions, lineage, reproducibility and falsifiability—not to create a false impression of valuation precision.
