# Reverse DCF interpretation

The Tencent and BYD native replays solve for one bounded multiplier on the complete base revenue-growth path while keeping the rest of the forward model unchanged.

This design guarantees that forward valuation and reverse market expectations use the same operating, reinvestment, discounting, terminal-value and equity-bridge rules.

The resulting first-year growth numbers are not stand-alone forecasts:

- Tencent: about 20.18% under the frozen base non-growth assumptions.
- BYD: about 95.49% under the frozen base non-growth assumptions.

The BYD result is intentionally not capped or softened. Its extremity is the diagnostic conclusion.
