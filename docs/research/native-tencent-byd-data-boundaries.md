# Native Tencent/BYD data boundaries

## Facts

Values transcribed from contemporaneous interim disclosures are stored separately from model policy.

## Normalization

- Tencent annualizes H1 revenue and infers weighted-average shares from profit/EPS.
- BYD annualizes H1 revenue and temporarily uses profit before tax as an operating-margin proxy.

## Scenario policy

Growth, margin, RONIC, WACC, stable growth, holding discounts and the 15% investor hurdle are research assumptions. They are never labeled as reported facts.

## Market expectations

Reverse solves change only the stated growth-path multiplier while reusing the same forward model. Reverse outputs are diagnostics and must not overwrite the scenario assumptions.

## Outcome data

The later realized-return proxies originate from the frozen legacy bridge. They are not used to choose or tune Phase A assumptions.

## Allocation control

Both native runs remain `provisional`; therefore the decision layer may classify the opportunity, but the result is ineligible for automatic capital allocation.
